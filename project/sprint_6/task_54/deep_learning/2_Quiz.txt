Quiz Answer Key

1. The specific fields identified as problematic by
   the tester are "agreementDateFrom" and "agreementDateTo"
   within the request parameters.

2. The core issue is that these date fields can currently
   be set to dates in the past, implying that an insurance
   policy could be acquired for past events.

3. The tester believes it's incorrect because it makes no
   logical sense to calculate the cost of an insurance
   policy for a past trip or event. Insurance is typically
   for future risks.

4. The tester uses the humorous analogy of "acquiring
   insurance for a trip to the past" to emphasize the illogical
   nature of the current system.

5. The tester consulted "the business" (бизнес) to clarify
   and confirm the expected behavior and validation rules
   for the dates.

6. The business confirmed that the calculation of insurance
   policy cost should not be based on past dates. Only future
   dates are permissible.

7. Only dates from the future (будущие даты) are explicitly
   stated as the only permissible dates for policy cost calculation.

8. The primary task assigned is to implement the corresponding
   validation logic to prevent the use of past dates in the
   specified fields.

9. The "PS" note indicates that the developer must also
   remember to correct all existing tests (исправить все тесты)
   when implementing the new validation.

10. Testing is considered an important stage because it
    helps identify errors and inconsistencies in the software,
    ensuring that the application functions as intended and
    meets business requirements.
