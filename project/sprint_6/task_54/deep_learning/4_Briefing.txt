Briefing Document: Insurance Policy Validation - Future Dates Only

Subject:
Addressing a critical bug identified during software
testing regarding insurance policy date validation.

1. Executive Summary

A significant logical error has been identified in
the insurance policy application where the agreementDateFrom
and agreementDateTo fields allow users to specify dates in
the past. This effectively permits the "purchase of insurance
for a trip to the past," which is illogical and incorrect from
a business perspective. Business stakeholders have confirmed
that insurance policy cost calculations should only be based
on future dates. The immediate task is to implement the
necessary validation to enforce this requirement and update
all associated tests.

2. Key Issues and Findings

The core issue revolves around the application's current
lack of validation for date fields, specifically:

agreementDateFrom: The start date of the insurance policy.

agreementDateTo: The end date of the insurance policy.

These fields currently accept past dates, leading to an
absurd scenario where "страховку можно приобрести на путешествие
в прошлое?" (insurance can be purchased for a trip to the past?).

3. Business Logic and Requirements

The business has provided clear guidance on the expected behavior:

No Past Dates: "Бизнес подтвердил, что расчёт стоимости
полиса страхования не должен основываться на датах из прошлого."
(The business confirmed that the calculation of the insurance
policy cost should not be based on dates from the past.)

Future Dates Only: "Допустимы только даты из будущего."
(Only future dates are permissible.)

Logical Premise: The underlying rationale is that
"рассчитывать стоимость полиса страхования в прошлом
не имеет смысла" (calculating the cost of an insurance
policy in the past makes no sense).

4. Required Action

The primary task is to implement robust validation for
the agreementDateFrom and agreementDateTo fields. This
validation must ensure that only future dates are accepted
for these parameters.

5. Impacted Areas

Codebase:
The specific module or function responsible for processing
the agreementDateFrom and agreementDateTo parameters in the
insurance policy request.

Test Suite:
All existing tests related to insurance policy creation,
date validation, and cost calculation will need to be reviewed
and updated to reflect the new "future dates only" requirement.
The source explicitly states: "не забудьте при реализации
исправить все тесты" (don't forget to fix all tests during
implementation).

6. Recommendations

Prioritize the implementation of the date validation logic
for agreementDateFrom and agreementDateTo.

Ensure the validation accurately identifies and rejects any past dates.

Thoroughly review and update all relevant unit, integration,
and end-to-end tests to confirm the new validation works as
expected and that no regressions are introduced.

Consider adding clear error messages to the user interface
when invalid (past) dates are entered.
