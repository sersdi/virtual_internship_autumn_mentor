Glossary of Key Terms

Тестировщик (Tester):
An individual responsible for identifying bugs, errors,
and inconsistencies in software applications by systematically
testing them.

Тестирование (Testing):
The process of evaluating a software application to detect
errors and ensure it meets specified requirements and expected
behavior.

Ошибка (Error/Bug):
A flaw or defect in a software program that causes it to
produce an incorrect or unexpected result, or to behave
in unintended ways.

Поля запроса (Request fields):
Specific parameters or data inputs that are sent as part
of a request to a software system, e.g., "agreementDateFrom"
and "agreementDateTo."

Прошлое (Past): Referring to a time that has already occurred.

Будущее (Future): Referring to a time that is yet to come.

Валидация (Validation):
The process of checking whether data inputs are clean,
correct, and useful for processing, ensuring they meet
predefined rules and constraints.

Расчет стоимости полиса (Policy cost calculation):
The process of determining the premium or price of an insurance
policy based on various factors.

Бизнес (Business):
In the context of software development, this often refers
to the stakeholders, product owners, or departments that
define the requirements and desired functionality of the software.

Реализовать (Implement):
To put a plan or design into effect; in software, to write
the code that brings a feature or fix to fruition.

Исправить все тесты (Fix all tests):
To update or correct existing automated tests to reflect
changes in the application's logic or requirements, ensuring
they continue to accurately verify functionality.
