Quiz

1. What is the primary characteristic that
   distinguishes a senior-level developer from others,
   according to the mentor?

2. Provide two specific examples of poor quality indicators
   that stem from the "It'll do!" approach mentioned in the text.

3. What is identified as the fundamental reason why most
   programmers produce inconsistent quality in their work?

4. Define what "personal quality standards" are in the
   context of the provided text.

5. What is the purpose of establishing personal quality
   standards for a developer?

6. List one specific example of a personal quality
   standard provided in the text.

7. What is the "Boy Scout rule" as described in the text,
   and what does it encourage developers to do?

8. What two ongoing actions are developers encouraged
   to take regarding their personal quality standards?

9. How does strictly adhering to personal quality
   standards help a developer stand out from the general mass?

10. What is the ultimate long-term outcome for a
    developer who consistently follows and improves
    their personal quality standards?


Quiz Answer Key

1. According to the mentor, the primary characteristic
   that distinguishes a senior-level developer from others
   is the quality of their work. They consistently strive
   for and achieve high standards in their development efforts.

2. Two specific examples of poor quality indicators stemming
   from the "It'll do!" approach are the "copy/paste" approach
   to code and the presence of dirty code. These practices
   often lead to unmaintainable and unreliable software.

3. The fundamental reason identified for inconsistent
   quality among most programmers is the absence of their
   own defined quality standards. Without these standards,
   their work quality can fluctuate significantly.

4. Personal quality standards are defined as a developer's
   self-imposed minimum level of quality, below which they
   will never allow their work to fall. They represent a
   commitment to consistently high standards.

5. The purpose of establishing personal quality standards
   is to ensure a consistent baseline of high-quality work
   and to prevent a developer from ever descending below
   that self-defined threshold of excellence.

6. One specific example of a personal quality standard
   provided is: "I will always write unit tests for my code."
   This ensures a basic level of code verification and reliability.

7. The "Boy Scout rule" encourages developers to always strive
   to improve the code they are working with, even if they
   are only fixing a small bug. It promotes leaving the
   codebase in a better state than they found it.

8. Developers are encouraged to both strictly monitor the
   adherence to their current standards and to regularly
   expand and enhance them. This ensures continuous
   improvement and growth.

9. Strictly adhering to personal quality standards helps
   a developer stand out by demonstrating a consistent
   commitment to excellence and continuous improvement.
   This sets them apart from those who produce inconsistent work.

10. The ultimate long-term outcome for a developer who
    consistently follows and improves their personal quality
    standards is achieving great heights in the art of
    software development. This implies reaching senior
    or even higher levels of expertise and recognition.
