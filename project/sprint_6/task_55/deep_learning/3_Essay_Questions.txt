Essay Questions

1. Discuss the "It'll Do!" approach in software development.
   What are its common manifestations, its underlying cause
   according to the mentor, and how do personal quality
   standards serve as a countermeasure?

2. Explain the concept of "personal quality standards"
   as presented in the text. How do they differ from external
   project requirements, and what is their significance for
   individual developer growth and the overall quality of
   software projects?

3. Analyze the role of continuous improvement in the context
   of personal quality standards. How does the mentor suggest
   developers maintain and enhance their standards, and what
   are the long-term benefits of this ongoing commitment?

4. Beyond the examples provided ("writing unit tests" and
   the "Boy Scout rule"), propose and elaborate on two
   additional personal quality standards a developer might
   adopt to improve their work. Justify why these standards
   would be beneficial.

5. Based on the text, argue why a consistent commitment to quality,
   driven by personal standards, is more impactful for career
   advancement to a "senior developer" level than simply
   accumulating years of experience or a broad range of
   technical skills.
