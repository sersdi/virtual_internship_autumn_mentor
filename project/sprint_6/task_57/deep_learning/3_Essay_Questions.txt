Essay Questions

1. Discuss the architectural benefits of creating dedicated
   packages for specific functionalities like validation.
   How does this align with principles of good software design,
   and what challenges might arise during such a refactoring
   effort in a large codebase?

2. Beyond the explicit reasons mentioned (many classes, grouping),
   elaborate on other potential motivations for undertaking the
   "Validation Classes Refactor" task. Consider aspects like
   testability, reusability, and onboarding new team members.

3. Imagine you are leading this refactoring effort.
   Outline a step-by-step plan for moving the validation classes,
   including considerations for version control, testing, and
   minimizing disruption to ongoing development.

4. Compare and contrast the impact of this refactoring if the
   validation classes were previously scattered across many
   different modules versus if they were already somewhat grouped
   but not in a dedicated "validations" package. How would the
   complexity and benefits differ?

5. What are the potential future implications of having a
   well-defined core/validations package? Consider how this
   structure might influence the introduction of new validation
   frameworks, rule engines, or cross-cutting concerns related
   to data integrity.
