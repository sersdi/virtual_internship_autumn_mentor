Essay Format Questions

1. Discuss the different methods for executing Gradle build
   commands described in the source material, detailing the
   advantages and disadvantages of each method in a development
   workflow.

2. Explain the critical role of the JAVA_HOME environment
   variable in enabling Gradle operations from the terminal.
   How does its absence impact the developer's ability to
   build projects?

3. Compare and contrast the process of performing a full
   project build versus building a single module in Gradle.
   When might a developer choose one over the other, and what
   are the implications for efficiency?

4. Describe the importance of managing dependencies in a Gradle
   project as hinted by the "Reload all Gradle projects"
   functionality. How does Gradle facilitate this, and what
   problems might arise if this step is neglected?

5. Analyze the overall workflow suggested by the instructions,
   from opening the Gradle tab to documenting the completed task.
   How does this structured approach contribute to effective
   project management and development?
