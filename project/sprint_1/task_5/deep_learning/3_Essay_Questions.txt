Essay Format Questions

1. Discuss the significance of the build folder's orange
   highlight in the Idea development environment.
   How does this visual cue contribute to a developer's
   workflow and understanding of a project's structure?

2. Imagine you are troubleshooting an issue where your
   application is not running correctly after a Gradle build.
   Describe how you would use the contents of the build/libs
   and build/classes/java/main folders to begin your investigation.

3. Explain the importance of separating production bytecode
   from test bytecode within the build/classes directory.
   What are the practical benefits of this organizational
   structure for developers and the build process?

4. The source mentions that the generated and resources
   folders will be explored in more detail later. Based on
   your general knowledge of software development, hypothesize
   what types of files you might expect to find in these folders
   and why they are necessary for an application.

5. Beyond simply exploring the build folder, the source emphasizes
   documenting the task. Discuss the broader importance of detailed
   documentation in a software development project, particularly
   in the context of build processes and artifacts.
