Essay Questions

Discuss the critical roles of build.gradle and settings.gradle
in defining and managing a multi-module Gradle project. How do
they complement each other in terms of project configuration and
structure?

Explain the concept of "dependencies" in the context of
Gradle projects and how Maven Central facilitates the
management of these dependencies. What would be the
implications of not having a repository like Maven Central?

Analyze the importance of specifying sourceCompatibility
and targetCompatibility in a Gradle project. How do these
settings impact development, compilation, and deployment,
and what are the potential consequences of misconfiguring them?

Describe the function and significance of Gradle plugins
based on the provided text. Using the 'java' and 'idea'
plugins as examples, elaborate on how they extend Gradle's
capabilities and simplify project management.

Discuss the benefits of using a standardized character
encoding like UTF-8 and a specific testing platform like
JUnit 5 in software development. How do these choices contribute
to project consistency, reliability, and collaboration?
