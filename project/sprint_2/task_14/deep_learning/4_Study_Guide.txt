Study Guide

This study guide is designed to help you understand best
practices in unit testing, particularly the principle of
"one assertion per test," and to review how java.util.Date
objects behave in Java.

Key Concepts to Master:

Unit Test Granularity:
Understanding why a single assertion per test method is considered ideal.

Test Failure Diagnosis:
How ideal tests facilitate quick and accurate identification
of root causes for failures.

Code Quality vs. Pragmatism:
The tension between ideal code/tests and real-world project constraints.

Learning Project Philosophy:
The importance of focusing on quality and ideal practices
in an educational setting.

Effective Work Habits:
The benefit of continuous, uninterrupted blocks of time
for focused work.

Problem-Solving Algorithm (Unknowns):
A systematic approach to addressing unknown technical details,
emphasizing documentation.

java.util.Date Behavior:
How Date objects store and represent time, and the
implications for rapid, sequential instantiation.
