Essay Questions

1. Compare and contrast the DateTimeServiceV1
   and DateTimeServiceV2 implementations, focusing
   on their design principles, reusability, and
   impact on system performance.

2. Discuss the concept of "state" in object-oriented
   programming. Explain how the presence or absence
   of state fundamentally alters how objects are used
   and their implications for application design,
   particularly concerning resource management.

3. Explain why the principles of statelessness are
   particularly important in the context of multithreaded
   applications. Use the provided text's hints about
   "thread safety" to elaborate on your answer.

4. Imagine you are designing a new software component.
   Describe a scenario where you would intentionally
   choose to implement a stateful object and another
   scenario where a stateless object would be the preferred
   design. Justify your choices with reasoning beyond just performance.

5. The text suggests that creating "extra" objects negatively
   impacts performance. Elaborate on the mechanisms by which
   object creation and deletion affect system resources,
   including CPU cycles and memory, and how this relates
   to the choice between stateless and stateful designs.
