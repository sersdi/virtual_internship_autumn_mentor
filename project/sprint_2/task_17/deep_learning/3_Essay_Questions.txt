Essay Format Questions

1. Discuss the pedagogical approach taken in this developer's
   guide, particularly the decision to start with "simple"
   tasks and introduce "fake values." How do these choices
   contribute to effective developer onboarding and project management?

2. Analyze the importance of the instruction "Good programmer
   should learn to feel when to write new code and when to
   find a ready-made solution." Provide examples of common
   programming tasks where finding a ready-made solution is
   preferable and explain why.

3. Elaborate on the emphasis placed on testing in this guide.
   Why is it crucial for developers to test their changes,
   modify existing tests, and ensure all tests pass before
   committing to the repository? What are the potential
   consequences of neglecting these steps?

4. Consider the concept of agreementPrice being a "fake value"
   initially. Discuss how this approach allows for iterative
   development and helps manage complexity in software projects.
   What are the benefits and potential drawbacks of using
   placeholders like this?

5. This guide touches upon several aspects beyond just coding,
   such as "communication with a mentor and colleagues."
   Discuss the role of non-technical skills like communication
   and collaboration in a successful software development team,
   as implied by this introductory task.
