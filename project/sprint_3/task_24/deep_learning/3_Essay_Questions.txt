Essay Format Questions

1. Compare and contrast Constructor Injection,
   Setter (Method) Injection, and Field (Property) Injection.
   Discuss the primary advantages and disadvantages of each,
   and explain why Constructor Injection is often considered
   the preferred method for mandatory dependencies.

2. Elaborate on how Dependency Injection contributes to
   writing more testable code. Provide specific examples
   of how DI facilitates unit testing compared to a system without DI.

3. Discuss the concept of "Inversion of Control" in detail.
   Explain how Dependency Injection is a manifestation of
   this principle and analyze the overall benefits IoC brings
   to software design, beyond just dependency management.

4. Imagine you are designing a new module for a complex
   application. Describe a scenario where you would intentionally
   choose Setter (Method) Injection over Constructor Injection,
   justifying your decision with specific technical reasons.
   What are the potential trade-offs in this scenario?

5. The article mentions that Field Injection is often
   considered "evil." Explain why this perception exists,
   detailing the specific problems and design flaws that arise
   from its use. Are there any niche scenarios where it might
   be considered acceptable, and if so, under what strict conditions?
