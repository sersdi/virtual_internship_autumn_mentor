Essay Format Questions

1. Discuss the benefits of using Inversion of Control and
   Dependency Injection in the context of large-scale
   software development. Provide specific examples of
   how these patterns improve maintainability, testability,
   and scalability.

2. Compare and contrast constructor dependency injection
   with other potential methods of dependency injection
   (e.g., setter injection, field injection, though not
   explicitly mentioned in the source, you can research
   them briefly for comparison). Explain why constructor
   injection is often considered a preferred method.

3. Explain the role of the Spring IoC container in managing
   the lifecycle of objects and resolving dependencies. How does
   it leverage annotations like @Component to achieve this?

4. Elaborate on the "separation of concerns" principle and how
   the advice to "extract algorithms into separate classes"
   aligns with this principle. Discuss the long-term advantages
   of adhering to this practice.

5. Imagine you are tasked with refactoring an existing monolithic
   application that does not use IoC/DI. Outline a step-by-step
   approach for introducing Spring's IoC and Dependency Injection
   principles into this application, focusing on how you would
   identify and address dependencies.
