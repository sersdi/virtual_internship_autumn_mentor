Essay Format Questions (No Answers Provided)

1. Discuss the relationship between a developer's personal
   values regarding their work and the tangible quality
   of the software they produce, drawing upon the arguments
   presented in the text.

2. Analyze the concept of "true mastery" as described in the text.
   What are its defining characteristics, and how does the text
   suggest one can achieve it in the field of software development?

3. The text suggests that a culture of not valuing work can
   exist within a company. How might such a culture develop,
   what are its potential negative impacts, and how could the
   principles outlined in the text counteract it?

4. Explore the challenges mentioned in the text (e.g.,
   deadlines, management pressure) that hinder the
   production of quality work. How does the text reframe
   these challenges into opportunities for individual
   growth and distinction?

5. Beyond the individual developer, the text claims that
   quality can "elevate entire companies." Using the example
   of Apple provided, elaborate on how a collective commitment
   to valuing the results of labor can lead to broader
   organizational success.
