Glossary of Key Terms

ИТ решения (IT Solutions):
Refers to the software, systems, and applications that
developers create to address specific problems or needs.

Код (Code):
The set of instructions written in a programming language
that forms a software program.

Тестируете (Testing):
The process of evaluating software to find and fix defects,
ensuring it functions correctly and meets requirements.

Качественные решения (Quality Solutions):
Software or IT products that are well-designed, reliable,
efficient, and meet high standards of performance and user experience.

Хороший код (Good Code):
Code that is clean, readable, maintainable, efficient,
and performs its intended function effectively, making
it pleasant to work with.

Ценить результаты своего труда (Value the Results of Your Labor):
To appreciate, respect, and feel a sense of ownership and pride
in the outcomes of one's work.

Вкладывать свою душу (Invest Your Soul):
To put deep personal effort, passion, and dedication into one's
work, going beyond mere obligation.

Настоящий мастер (True Master):
An individual who demonstrates exceptional skill, expertise,
and a consistent commitment to excellence in their craft,
distinguished by the high quality of their work.

Культура, которая не поощряет качество
(Culture That Does Not Encourage Quality):
A workplace environment or organizational ethos where the
pursuit of high-quality work is not valued, rewarded,
or even actively discouraged, often in favor of speed or cost.

Мастерство (Mastery):
The comprehensive knowledge, skill, and expertise in a
particular field, achieved through consistent practice,
dedication, and a commitment to excellence.
