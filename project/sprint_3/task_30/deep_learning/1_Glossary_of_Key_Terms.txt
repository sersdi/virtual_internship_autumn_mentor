Glossary of Key Terms

Бизнес требование (Business Requirement):
A specific condition or capability that a system must meet
or possess to satisfy a contract, standard, specification,
or other formally imposed document. In this context, it refers
to the rule about surname validation.

Валидный (Valid):
Meeting the necessary conditions or requirements; acceptable.
A "valid" request adheres to all specified rules.

Не валидный (Invalid):
Not meeting the necessary conditions or requirements;
unacceptable. An "invalid" request fails to adhere to
one or more specified rules (e.g., missing a surname).

Реализуйте (Implement):
To put a decision or plan into effect; to build or
code the functionality. In this context, it means
writing the code to enforce the business rule.

Тесты (Tests):
Procedures or sets of instructions designed to verify
that a piece of software or a system functions correctly
and meets its specifications. They are crucial for ensuring
quality and correctness.

Документация (Documentation):
Written materials that record the details of a system,
project, or process. In this context, it refers to collecting
and organizing system requirements.

Требования к системе (System Requirements):
Detailed specifications of what a software system must
do and what properties it must have. These include functional
and non-functional requirements.

Разработка проекта (Project Development):
The ongoing process of creating and refining a software
application or system, from initial conception to deployment
and maintenance.
