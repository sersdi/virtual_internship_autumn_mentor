Essay Questions

Discuss the advantages of integrating Mockito with
JUnit for unit testing. How does this integration
streamline the testing process compared to manual mock
object creation and management?

Explain the lifecycle of a test when
@ExtendWith(MockitoExtension.class) is used.
What steps does the extension perform "under the hood"
to facilitate testing with mocks?

Compare and contrast the responsibilities of the
@Mock and @InjectMocks annotations. Illustrate their
combined use with a hypothetical scenario, explaining
why both are necessary.

Beyond the specific functionalities mentioned, what
broader principles of effective unit testing are
supported by the use of mock objects and a framework like Mockito?

Consider a situation where a developer chooses not to
use @ExtendWith(MockitoExtension.class) but still
wants to use Mockito. Describe the potential challenges
and additional steps they would need to take to achieve
similar results.
