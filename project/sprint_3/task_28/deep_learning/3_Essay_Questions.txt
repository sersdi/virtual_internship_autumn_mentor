Essay Questions

1. Discuss the critical importance of input data validation
   in the broader context of IT solution development.
   Why is it considered indispensable, especially in
   applications with significant human interaction?

2. Analyze the role of human error in driving the necessity
   for robust input data validation. Provide specific examples
   of common human mistakes and explain how validation
   mechanisms address them.

3. Elaborate on the challenges involved in implementing
   effective input data validation. Considering the mentor's
   provision of a prototype, what might be the complexities
   that necessitate such a starting point rather than a simple
   directive?

4. Describe the process a developer would undertake when
   incorporating a validation prototype, such as the one
   provided by the mentor, into their project. What are the
   key steps, considerations, and potential benefits or drawbacks
   of using a prototype?

5. Imagine an IT solution without proper input data validation.
   Discuss the potential negative consequences for the application's
   functionality, data integrity, user experience, and overall
   system reliability.
