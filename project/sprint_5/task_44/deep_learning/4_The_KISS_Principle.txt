The KISS Principle: Simplicity in Design

What does KISS stand for?
KISS stands for "Keep It Simple, Stupid."

What is the core idea of the KISS principle?
The core idea of the KISS principle is that designs, solutions,
systems, and products function best when they are kept simple.
It advocates for favoring simplicity and actively avoiding
unnecessary complexity.

Where is the KISS principle primarily applied?
The provided source specifically mentions its application
as a software design principle.

What does the KISS principle advise regarding complexity?
The KISS principle advises against complexity as much as possible,
suggesting that simplicity should be preferred over complex approaches.

Is the KISS principle only about software?
While the source specifically discusses it as a software
design principle, the general phrasing "designs, solutions,
systems, and products" suggests a broader applicability beyond
just software, implying it's a general principle that finds
strong resonance in software development.

Why is simplicity favored according to the KISS principle?
Simplicity is favored because, according to the principle,
designs, solutions, systems, and products "work best if they're
kept simple."

What is the primary directive of the KISS principle?
The primary directive of the KISS principle is to favor simplicity
over complexity and to avoid complexity whenever possible.

How does the KISS principle guide development or design?
It guides development and design by urging creators to always
seek the simplest viable solution or design, rather than
overcomplicating things.
