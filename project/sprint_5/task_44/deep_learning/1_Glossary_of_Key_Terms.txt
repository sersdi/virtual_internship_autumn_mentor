Glossary of Key Terms

KISS Principle:
An acronym standing for "Keep It Simple, Stupid," which is a
design principle.

Simplicity:
The quality or state of being easy to understand or do;
lack of complexity. In this context, it is favored.

Complexity:
The state or quality of being intricate or complicated.
In this context, it should be avoided as much as possible.

Software Design Principle:
A fundamental guideline or rule used in the development
and structure of software.

Designs:
The process or act of planning and creating something.
In this context, it refers to the blueprint or structure
of software, systems, or products.

Solutions:
A means of solving a problem or dealing with a difficult situation.

Systems:
A set of principles or procedures according to which
something is done; an organized scheme or method.

Products:
An article or substance that is manufactured or refined for sale.
In this context, it refers to the final output of a design or
development process.
