Glossary of Key Terms

yyyy-MM-dd:
A standard date format where "yyyy" represents the four-digit year,
"MM" represents the two-digit month, and "dd" represents the
two-digit day. The components are separated by hyphens.

Запрос (Zapros):
Russian for "query." In the context of databases and software,
a query is a request for data or information from a database or system.

Формат даты (Format daty):
Russian for "date format." Refers to the specific structure
and order in which date components (year, month, day) are represented.

Установить (Ustanovit'):
Russian for "to set" or "to establish." Implies an action
of defining or configuring something.

Явно (Yavno):
Russian for "explicitly," "clearly," or "plainly."
Emphasizes that something must be stated directly and
not left to assumption or default.

Спецификация (Spetsifikatsiya):
Russian for "specification." A detailed description of
the requirements, design, behavior, or other characteristics
of a system, component, or process.

Ваша задача (Vasha zadacha):
Russian for "Your task." Indicates a direct instruction
or assignment given to the user.
