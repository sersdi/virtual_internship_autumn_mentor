Essay Questions

1. Discuss the critical distinction between planning test
   scenarios and implementing test code. Explain why
   maintaining this separation of concerns is vital
   for achieving comprehensive and effective test coverage.

2. Imagine you are a mentor guiding a junior developer
   on testing practices. Explain the potential pitfalls
   of a "just start writing tests" approach and articulate
   the benefits of first crafting a detailed list of test
   scenarios, using a concrete example to illustrate your points.

3. The source material emphasizes the importance of covering
   "all application logic." Propose a methodology or set of steps
   you would follow to ensure your list of test scenarios for a
   given application genuinely achieves this comprehensive coverage,
   particularly for an application with "simple" logic.

4. Consider the TravelCalculatePremiumController as the target
   for testing. Based on the principles outlined in the source,
   design a hypothetical structure or content for a test scenario
   document for this controller. Justify your chosen format and
   content elements.

5. Evaluate the statement: "Working over the list of test scenarios,
   you will concentrate on what needs to be tested, what business
   case to cover with a test. Working further on the implementation
   of the tests, you will think about the technical aspects of
   writing test code." Discuss the implications of this dual
   focus on the quality and maintainability of a software project.
