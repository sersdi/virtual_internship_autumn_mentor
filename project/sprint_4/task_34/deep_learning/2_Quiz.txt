Quiz: Travel Date Validation

1. What is the primary business requirement stated for travel dates?

2. Which date must come after the other, according to the rule?

3. What is your specific task regarding agreementDateFrom
   and agreementDateTo?

4. Why is it explicitly mentioned that "solution
   without tests will not be accepted"?

5. If agreementDateFrom is January 10th and
   agreementDateTo is January 9th, would this scenario
   pass the validation? Explain why or why not.

6. Describe what "validation" means in the context
   of this business requirement.

7. What kind of error would likely occur if this
   business rule were not implemented?

8. Why is testing considered a crucial part of
   the development process for this task?

9. Can agreementDateTo be the same as agreementDateFrom
   according to the stated rule? Explain your answer.

10. What does the term "business rule" signify in the
    context of this problem?


Quiz Answer Key

1. The primary business requirement is that the trip
   end date (agreementDateTo) must be strictly after
   the trip start date (agreementDateFrom). This ensures
   logical temporal order for travel periods.

2. According to the rule, agreementDateTo (the end date)
   must come after agreementDateFrom (the start date).
   This establishes the sequence of the travel period.

3. My specific task is to implement validation logic
   to ensure that agreementDateFrom is strictly less
   than agreementDateTo. This means writing code that
   checks this condition.

4. It is explicitly mentioned that "solution without
   tests will not be accepted" to underscore the critical
   importance of verifying the correctness and robustness
   of the implemented logic. Tests prove that the rule is
   properly enforced.

5. No, if agreementDateFrom is January 10th and agreementDateTo
   is January 9th, this scenario would not pass validation.
   The rule states that the end date must be after the start date,
   and in this case, it is before.

6. In this context, "validation" means checking that the input
   travel dates (agreementDateFrom and agreementDateTo) conform
   to the defined business rule. It's the process of ensuring
   data quality and logical consistency.

7. If this business rule were not implemented, it could lead
   to illogical or invalid travel bookings where the trip
   ends before it begins. This would result in data integrity
   issues and potential operational problems.

8. Testing is considered crucial because it verifies that
   the implemented validation logic works as intended and
   catches any errors or edge cases. It ensures the reliability
   and correctness of the business rule enforcement.

9. No, agreementDateTo cannot be the same as agreementDateFrom
   according to the stated rule. The phrasing "должна быть после"
   (must be after) implies a strict inequality, meaning the end
   date must be later, not equal to, the start date.

10. In this problem, "business rule" signifies a specific policy
    or condition that governs how data related to travel dates
    must behave. It's a fundamental requirement set by the business
    for logical and valid operations.
