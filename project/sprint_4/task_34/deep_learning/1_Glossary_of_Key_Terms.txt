Glossary of Key Terms

agreementDateFrom:
Represents the start date of a trip or agreement.

agreementDateTo:
Represents the end date of a trip or agreement.

Business Rule:
A specific condition or principle that governs a
particular aspect of a business process or data.
In this context, it's the requirement that the end date
must be after the start date.

Validation:
The process of checking data to ensure it meets certain
criteria or rules (e.g., format, range, logical consistency)
before it is processed or stored.

Testing:
The process of evaluating a system or its component(s)
with the intent to find whether it satisfies the specified
requirements or to identify defects. For this task, it's
explicitly required to prove the validation logic works.
