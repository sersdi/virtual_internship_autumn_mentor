Date Logic Validation and Testing Requirements

What is the core business requirement described?

The core business requirement is that the "agreement end date"
(agreementDateTo) must be after the "agreement start date"
(agreementDateFrom). In simpler terms, a trip cannot end before
it begins.

What specific validation needs to be implemented?

Validation must be added to ensure that agreementDateFrom
is less than agreementDateTo. This directly enforces the
business rule that the start date precedes the end date.

Is testing a critical component of this task?

Yes, testing is explicitly stated as critical. The source
emphasizes, "Don't forget about tests! A solution without
tests is not accepted!" This indicates that the implementation
must be accompanied by a robust set of tests to verify its correctness.

What are the key variables involved in this validation?

The key variables involved are agreementDateFrom
(the agreement or trip start date) and agreementDateTo
(the agreement or trip end date).

What programming concept does this task relate to?

This task relates directly to input validation and data
integrity in software development. It's about ensuring that
user-provided or system-generated dates conform to logical
and business constraints.

Why is it important to implement this business rule?

Implementing this business rule is crucial for maintaining
data consistency and preventing illogical or invalid entries
in a system. Without it, a system could record trips or
agreements that defy the natural flow of time, leading to
errors in scheduling, reporting, or other dependent processes.

What is the expected outcome of a successful implementation?

A successful implementation will ensure that any attempt to
set agreementDateTo as earlier than or equal to agreementDateFrom
will be rejected or flagged as an error. Conversely, valid date
ranges (where the end date is after the start date) will be accepted.

What is the overall goal of this task?

The overall goal is to implement a fundamental business
rule related to date sequencing, ensure its correctness
through validation, and provide comprehensive tests to
guarantee the reliability of the solution.
