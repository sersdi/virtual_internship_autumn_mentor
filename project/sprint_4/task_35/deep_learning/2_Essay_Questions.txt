Essay Format Questions

1. Discuss the multi-faceted definition of a "good IT solution"
   presented in the text. How does this definition extend beyond
   mere functional requirements, and why are these additional
   qualities crucial for professional programming?

2. Explain the "Boy Scout Rule" in detail, both in its
   original context and as adapted by Robert C. Martin
   for programming. Provide arguments for why adhering
   to this rule is essential for maintaining and improving
   code quality over time, even when dealing with legacy code.

3. Analyze the concept of refactoring as described in
   the source. What is its core definition and primary goal?
   Furthermore, explain how refactoring contributes to the
   long-term health of a codebase and supports future
   development efforts.

4. The text states, "Positive changes over time accumulate,
   just as negative changes do." Elaborate on this statement,
   providing examples of how both continuous improvement and
   neglect can impact a software project's codebase over an
   extended period.

5. Imagine you are a mentor to a new programmer. Based on the
   provided text, what key advice would you give them regarding
   their responsibility for code quality and the importance of
   continuous improvement? Include specific principles and
   resources mentioned in the material.
