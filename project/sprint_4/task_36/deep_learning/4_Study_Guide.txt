Underwriting Process and Code Alignment Study Guide

I. Understanding the Core Business Process: Underwriting

The primary source defines "underwriting" as the process
of calculating the price of an insurance policy.

This involves assessing the risk associated with insuring
an individual or entity. Key factors considered during risk
assessment include age, health, occupation, location, and other
pertinent information. The ultimate goal of underwriting is to
determine a fair and sustainable premium for both the insurer
and the policyholder, based on the likelihood and potential
cost of a claim.

II. Analyzing the Current Code vs. Business Process Alignment

The current code, specifically TravelCalculatePremiumServiceImpl,
is identified as problematic because it doesn't clearly reflect
the core business process of underwriting. The crucial calculation
of the insurance policy cost (underwriting) is happening implicitly
during the creation of the response, rather than as a distinct
and prominent part of the code. The mentor emphasizes that
underwriting is the main purpose of the application, and the
code should explicitly represent this.

III. Goal of the Refactoring Effort

The main objective is to refactor the
TravelCalculatePremiumServiceImpl code to better
align with the described underwriting business process.

This means making the underwriting (insurance premium calculation)
explicit and central within the code, reflecting its importance
as the core function of the application. The refactoring should
aim to make the code's structure mirror the logical steps of the
business process.

IV. Key Considerations for Refactoring

Explicitness:
The calculation of the insurance premium (underwriting)
should be clearly identifiable and isolated within the code.

Centrality:
Underwriting should be presented as the primary operation,
not an incidental step.

Business Process Mirroring:
The code structure should ideally reflect the logical
flow of risk assessment and premium determination.

Testing:
Any changes to the code must be accompanied by updates
to the corresponding tests to ensure continued functionality
and correctness.
