Underwriting: Business Process Alignment in Code

What is underwriting in the context of insurance?

Underwriting is the process of calculating the price of an
insurance policy. It involves assessing the risk of insuring
a particular individual or entity by evaluating factors such
as age, health, occupation, location, and other relevant information.

What is the purpose of underwriting?

The purpose of underwriting is to determine the appropriate
premium to charge for a policy. This is based on the
likelihood of a claim being made and the potential cost
of that claim, ensuring premiums are fair and sustainable
for both the insurer and the policyholder.

Why is underwriting considered a key part of the insurance industry?

Underwriting is crucial because it ensures that premiums are
set at a level that accurately reflects the risk involved,
thereby balancing the financial viability for the insurer
with affordability for the policyholder.
