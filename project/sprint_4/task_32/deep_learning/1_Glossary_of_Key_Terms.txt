Glossary of Key Terms

agreementDateTo (дата окончания поездки):
The travel end date. This is the specific field that
requires validation.

Бизнес требование (Business Requirement):
A statement describing a condition or capability that
a solution must meet. In this context, it's the rule
that a travel request must have an end date.

валидация (Validation):
The process of checking whether input data is correct,
meaningful, and secure. Here, it refers to verifying the
presence of "agreementDateTo."

запрос (Request):
A formal communication or data packet submitted to a system.
In this context, it refers to a travel request.

не валидным (invalid):
Not meeting the specified criteria or rules; unacceptable.
A request without an "agreementDateTo" is considered invalid.

Решение без тестов не принимается!
(Solution without tests is not accepted!):
A critical directive emphasizing that the implementation
must be accompanied by comprehensive tests to prove its functionality.
