Essay Format Questions

1. Compare and contrast the OSI model and the TCP/IP
   protocol stack. Discuss their similarities, differences,
   and why both are still relevant in understanding modern networking.

2. Choose one network layer (e.g., Transport, Network,
   or Data Link) and describe its primary functions and
   the importance of those functions for overall network
   communication. Provide examples of protocols that operate
   at this layer.

3. Explain how the TCP and IP protocols work together to
   enable reliable data transmission over the internet.
   Discuss the specific roles each protocol plays in this process.

4. Why is it crucial for applications developed by programmers
   to communicate over networks? Discuss how network isolation
   would limit the functionality and utility of modern software,
   providing specific examples.

5. Describe the concept of "layers" in network models.
   Why is a layered approach beneficial for network design
   and development, and what advantages does it offer over
   a monolithic design?
