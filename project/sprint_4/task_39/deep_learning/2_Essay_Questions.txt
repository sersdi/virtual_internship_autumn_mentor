Essay Format Questions

1. Discuss the core principles of REST architecture and
   explain how they contribute to the scalability and
   maintainability of distributed systems.

2. Elaborate on the significance of "statelessness"
   in RESTful systems. How does it simplify server design
   and improve system reliability?

3. Compare and contrast the use of HTTP GET and POST
   methods in a REST API. Provide examples of scenarios
   where each would be the appropriate choice.

4. Explain how the consistent mapping of HTTP methods
   (GET, POST, PUT, DELETE) to CRUD operations simplifies
   the development and understanding of RESTful APIs.

5. Beyond the specific HTTP methods and URL structure,
   what other aspects contribute to a well-designed
   RESTful API, and why are they important?
